Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Information We Collect

Block Blast 2026 stores game progress, settings, scores, and power-up balances locally on your device.

The game uses anonymous Firebase Authentication to create a technical user identifier. You are not required to provide an email address, password, or real name.

If you submit a leaderboard score, your chosen nickname, score, cleared lines, anonymous user identifier, and submission time may be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Your nickname and score may be visible to other players on the public leaderboard.

Analytics Information

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve gameplay, performance, and features. Analytics information may include app interactions, device information, operating system details, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising or installation identifiers where permitted.

Advertising and Consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertisements.

Where required, we request your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. On iOS, we also request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency system before accessing the advertising identifier. You may decline tracking permission and still use the game. Depending on your consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where available, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices using the Privacy Options control in the game's settings. iOS tracking permission can also be changed in your device settings.

How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • Save game progress and provide gameplay features;
  • Operate and display the leaderboard;
  • Measure game usage and improve the player experience;
  • Display and measure advertisements;
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and technical problems;
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Third-Party Services

We use services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

You can learn more by reviewing the Google Privacy Policy .

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help us operate analytics, leaderboards, consent management, and advertising. Leaderboard nicknames and scores are intentionally visible to other players.

We may also disclose information where required by law or when necessary to protect our users, rights, or services.

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the application's data or uninstall the game.

Leaderboard and other cloud data may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the game, maintain leaderboard integrity, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. You may contact us to request deletion of eligible cloud data.

Google and other service providers may retain information according to their own retention policies.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing.

You can manage advertising consent through the game's Privacy Options control when available. You can manage iOS tracking permission through Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. These countries may have different data protection laws.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, services, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

To exercise your privacy rights or ask questions about this policy, contact us using the developer contact information provided on the game's App Store or Google Play listing.
