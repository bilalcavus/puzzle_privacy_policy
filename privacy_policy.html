01Information we collect

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, levels, scores, statistics, settings, daily rewards and in-game item balances locally on your device. This information is not sent to us.

Anonymous player identifier

The game uses anonymous Firebase Authentication to create a random technical identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your name, email address or phone number.

Leaderboard information

If you take part in the online leaderboard, we store the nickname you choose, your score and related game results, your anonymous identifier and the time of submission in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Your nickname and score are visible to other players. Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your nickname.

Usage and device information

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. This can include in-game events (for example levels started or completed, and ads viewed), session length, device model, operating system and version, language, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app instance or advertising identifiers where permitted.

Advertising information

Our advertising partners may collect device information, IP address, advertising identifiers (such as the Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID, only where permitted), ad interactions and diagnostic data to show and measure ads. See Advertising & consent.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your device. No push token or notification content is sent to us.

02How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and provide features such as daily rewards;
  • To operate the online leaderboard;
  • To understand how the game is used, fix problems and improve gameplay and difficulty;
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to measure their performance;
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, invalid ad activity and abuse;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Where EU, UK or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and analytics where consent is required, and on our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the game for the rest.

03Advertising & consent

The game is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may request ads from other advertising networks (listed in Third-party services) to find the best available ad. These networks receive the information needed to deliver and measure that ad.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads and analytics use your data for personalisation. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can decline and still play the full game. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Android

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path may vary by device).

You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time from Privacy options in the game's settings, where available in your region.

05Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • With the service providers listed above, to operate analytics, the leaderboard, consent management and advertising;
  • With other players, limited to your leaderboard nickname and score;
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, the game or others.

Under some US state laws, allowing ad networks to use device data for personalised advertising may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out using the in-game Privacy options, your device's ad settings, or by declining tracking on iOS.

06Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is operated, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics data: event-level data linked to identifiers is kept for 2 months, and user-level data for 14 months after your last activity in the game. It is then deleted automatically. Aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.
  • Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

07Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Ad consent: Privacy options in the game's settings (where available).
  • Tracking on iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: your device's ad settings.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your device's notification settings.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the game.
  • Delete leaderboard data: email 1-404radar@gmail.com.

Because we don't ask for your name or email, we may need your leaderboard nickname to locate your data when you contact us.

08Children's privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

09Security & international transfers

We use reputable service providers and reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, our partners or legal requirements change. The latest version is always published on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we may also notify you in the game.
