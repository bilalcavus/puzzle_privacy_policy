Privacy Policy

Collected Data

Your progress, scores, and the optional nickname you provide are stored locally. Analytics and crash reports stay anonymous and aren't linked to device identifiers.

Data Usage

We use this information to improve gameplay, fix technical issues, and plan new features. Data is never shared with advertisers.

Retention Period

Local saves remain on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, data is stored until you delete your account or stay inactive for 24 months.

Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact support via the in-app feedback panel to exercise these rights.
